PREETI'S 18TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION — FINAL PACKAGE

FILES
- index.html: all scenes and page structure
- style.css: responsive design and animations
- script.js: countdown, calendar, interactions, candles, cake cutting and final link
- README.md: local preview and publishing instructions

QUICK TEST
1. Extract this ZIP into a new folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. To preview scenes immediately, open index.html?preview=1 in a browser.
4. Use the PREVIEW arrows to move between scenes.

IMPORTANT
- The countdown is set for 18 November 2026 at 12:00 AM India time.
- The cake is stationary; it does not spin or respond to drag rotation.
- Candle tapping, the blow-candles button, draggable cake knife, optional synthesized tones, and Wonderland interactions are included.
- The final surprise button opens the existing PREETI-BIRTHDAY-WORLD website in a new tab.
- No MP3/WAV files, external fonts, CDNs, frameworks, or remote assets are required.
- Keep your original folder as a backup before replacing anything in GitHub Pages.
